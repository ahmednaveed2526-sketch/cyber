What happened?

The user clicked a link in an email and entered credentials on a page that looked legitimate but was fake. In a real attack, those details would be sent to the attacker.

How to protect yourself

  • Check the sender email carefully (look for typos and odd domains).
  • Hover over links to see the real destination before clicking.
  • Look for HTTPS and certificates, but don't rely on them alone.
  • When in doubt, navigate directly to the website rather than clicking email links.
