1. What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks. These attacks often aim to access, change, or destroy sensitive information or interrupt normal operations.

2. Understanding Phishing Attacks

Phishing is when cybercriminals send fake emails or create websites pretending to be trusted organizations. Their goal is to trick people into revealing sensitive information like passwords or credit card numbers.

Tip: Always check the website URL and never share your login info unless you are sure the site is genuine.

3. Password Safety

Using strong passwords is one of the easiest ways to protect your accounts. A strong password should be at least 12 characters long, combining letters, numbers, and special symbols.

Tip: Avoid using the same password on multiple websites.

4. Ethical Hacking

Ethical hackers use their technical skills to help organizations find and fix security vulnerabilities. Unlike malicious hackers, they work to protect systems, not exploit them.

Example: Penetration testers simulate cyberattacks to identify weaknesses before real hackers can find them.

5. Protecting Yourself Online

  • Keep your software updated.
  • Don’t click on suspicious links or attachments.
  • Use two-factor authentication (2FA) whenever possible.
  • Be cautious when using public Wi-Fi networks.
